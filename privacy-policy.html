Privacy Policy

App: Zaznamity
Developer: whitenight21
Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Zaznamity mobile application ("the App"), created by whitenight21 ("we," "our," or "us"). It explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect and How Data Is Stored

Zaznamity is a personal finance and budgeting application. Financial records and configuration settings you enter—such as transactions, balances, account pools, labels, savings/liability details, scenarios, and display or theme preferences—are stored locally on your device (for example, via a local database such as SQLite and local device preferences).

We do not require you to create an account with us to use core budgeting features, and we do not operate a central developer server to harvest or sell your personal financial entries.

2. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

Any third-party libraries, operating system services, or external links used by the App operate under their own terms and privacy policies. If third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are enabled in a release of Zaznamity, they will be disclosed here and matched with our Google Play Data Safety declaration. Where no ad or behavioral tracking SDK is included, the App does not serve behavioral ads or sell user data.

3. Exports, Backups, and Data Sharing

If you export reports (such as PDF) or back up/share files from your device, those files are generated or shared only when you initiate that action and choose the destination or recipient.

4. Security

Because your budgeting data is stored locally on your device, protecting your device (such as using a screen lock and device encryption) helps keep your local data safe. Where network connections are used (for example, opening external links or future optional connected features), data in transit is protected using modern encryption such as HTTPS.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your local data remains on your device until you edit it, delete items inside the App, clear the App's storage/cache in your device settings, or uninstall the App. You may also contact us using the details below with privacy or data deletion inquiries.

6. Children's Privacy

Zaznamity is intended for adult and general personal finance management and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or legal/store requirements. Updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
