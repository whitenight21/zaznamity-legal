Part 1: Privacy Policy

1. Local Storage & No Account Required

Zaznamity ("the App") is a personal finance, budgeting, and scenario-modeling application created by whitenight21 ("we," "our," or "us"). Data you enter into the App—such as transactions, balances, account pools, labels, liabilities, savings goals, what-if scenarios, and theme or currency preferences—is stored locally on your device (for example, in a local database such as SQLite and local device preferences).

You do not need to create an account with us to use the App, and we do not operate a developer backend server to collect, sell, or monetize your personal budgeting records.

2. Analytics, Crash Reporting & Advertising

At this time, Zaznamity does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services (such as Firebase Crashlytics), or advertising networks. If we add any third-party SDKs or optional connected services in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and align our store declarations accordingly.

3. Exports, Backups & Sharing

When you generate or export files (such as PDF summaries or backups) on your device, those files are created locally and shared or saved only when you choose to export or send them through your device or chosen third-party apps.

4. Security, Retention & Deletion

Because your data resides on your device, your device's operating system security (such as screen lock and full-disk encryption) is key to protecting local storage. You can edit or delete records inside the App at any time, or remove all local App data by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for personal finance tracking and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
